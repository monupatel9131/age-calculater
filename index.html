How age is calculated

MP Age Calculator uses calendar-aware arithmetic for the primary age result rather than simply dividing a number of days by 365.

1. Start with the date of birth

Your selected birth date becomes the starting point for the calculation.

2. Determine the target date

The current local date is used for the main calculator, while the Age on Any Date tool uses the date you choose.

3. Calculate calendar years, months and days

The calculator accounts for different month lengths and leap years. This is why an exact calendar age can differ from a simple day conversion.

Why isn't one month always 30 days?

Calendar months contain different numbers of days — typically 28, 29, 30 or 31. Therefore, a calendar age such as 20 years, 4 months and 12 days should not be treated as a fixed number of days using a single monthly conversion.

Duration statistics

Total days are measured using elapsed calendar-day boundaries. Hours, minutes and seconds are duration-based conversions and can vary when a time of birth is not supplied.
