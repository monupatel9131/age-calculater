Privacy-Focused QR Generation

The core QR generation process runs directly in your browser. QR information is not intentionally sent to an MP QR Tool server. Recent history and favorites, when used, are stored locally with your browser's localStorage.

Uploaded logos are processed locally for QR composition. If this website uses external CDN resources, advertising services or other third-party resources, those services may have their own data and privacy practices. Review their policies before relying on them for sensitive information.

Important: Do not put passwords, private keys, financial credentials or other highly sensitive information into QR codes unless you understand the security implications.

Before You Publish

A QR code simply encodes information. Always check that the encoded information is correct and scan the finished code using your own phone.

  • Check website URLs for spelling.
  • Verify UPI ID, recipient and amount before payment.
  • Keep sufficient contrast between QR modules and background.
  • Keep logos small enough to preserve scanability.
  • Test printed QR codes under the actual lighting and distance where they will be used.
